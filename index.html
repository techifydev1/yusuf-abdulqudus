    About

    I became an engineer partly out of spite for manual reconciliation: copying rows from a wallet into a spreadsheet at midnight isn’t a skill, it’s a bug in someone’s workflow. So I own problems from the first figma frame to the last deployed endpoint, and I care about the boring, hard bits that make software feel considered rather than just built.

    Right now that pain is Onreco: it watches your wallets, turns on-chain USDT/USDC into normal journal entries, and drops them into QuickBooks or Xero so accountants never have to say “blockchain” out loud.
